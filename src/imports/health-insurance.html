Health Insurance

Because a medical emergency shouldn't become a financial one.

Health insurance is a policy that covers hospitalization and medical expenses in exchange for a premium. Instead of paying out of pocket during a health emergency, your insurer settles the bill — directly with the hospital, or by reimbursing you. It protects your savings from being wiped out by a single medical event, rather than building wealth like an investment product.

Add-Ons

Riders worth considering

These are common industry-standard riders shown for reference — we'll confirm exactly which ones MyAnmol offers before this goes live.

Critical Illness Rider

Pays a lump sum on diagnosis of a covered illness like cancer or a heart condition, on top of your base cover.

Maternity Cover

Covers delivery and newborn care expenses, usually after a waiting period of 2–4 years.

Personal Accident Cover

An additional payout for accidental injury or disability, beyond standard hospitalization cover.

Before You Buy

What to check before you buy

✓

Network Hospitals

Check that hospitals near you are included, so you can actually use cashless treatment when it matters.

✓

Waiting Period

Most policies have a waiting period for pre-existing conditions — understand exactly how long before you're fully covered.

✓

Room Rent Sub-Limits

Some policies cap the room rent they'll cover, which can reduce your payout even within the sum insured.

✓

Claim Settlement Ratio & No-Claim Bonus

A strong settlement track record, plus a bonus that increases your cover each claim-free year.

Set the Record Straight

Common myths about health insurance

Myth
"My employer's group cover is enough."
Group cover usually ends the day you leave the job, and the sum insured is often too low to handle a serious illness — a personal policy fills that gap.
Myth
"I'm young and healthy, I don't need it."
Premiums are lowest and waiting periods start earliest when you buy young — plus you're covered before any health issue can be called "pre-existing."
Myth
"Health insurance covers everything."
Most policies exclude certain treatments, have waiting periods, and cap room rent — reading the fine print matters as much as buying the policy.
